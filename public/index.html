Safe processing

  • One registration at a time.
  • Provider rate limits pause the global worker.
  • Transient failures use exponential backoff and jitter.
  • Policy/abuse responses open a permanent circuit for manual review.
  • Credentials remain isolated per inbox and are never shown here.
  • Live phase, elapsed time, heartbeat and ETA are shown while a registration is running.
